## The problem

A growing coffee chain lost customers to the morning queue. Their old app was a web view that forgot your order, logged you out and froze on weak mall Wi-Fi.

## What I built

A native-feeling Flutter app for iOS and Android. Customers save a usual order, pick a pickup time and pay before they leave the office. The barista screen shows orders in the sequence people will actually arrive.

## How

Riverpod keeps state predictable across the menu, cart and checkout. Orders queue locally and sync when the signal returns, so a dropped connection never loses a paid order. Animations stay at 60fps on four-year-old Android phones, which I tested on real devices, not just the simulator.

## Result

Mobile orders passed walk-in orders at two outlets within three months. The app holds a 4.8 rating, and the most common review mentions that it simply remembers what you like.
