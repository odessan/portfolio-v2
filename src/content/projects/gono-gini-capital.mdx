## The problem

A systematic fund needed to show prospective investors how it runs and what it has actually returned, without hype. The pitch is low risk and steady growth, so the site had to feel as calm and boring as the strategy.

## What I built

A single-page marketing site with the desk's three principles up front: systematic, mathematical, intentionally boring. Below that sits a track record section with all-time return, average month, positive months and months tracked. A projection tool lets visitors see how an initial investment could have performed on the fund's monthly history. An earnings calendar shows actual daily fund returns, month by month.

## Investor access

Alongside the web app, I built the Flutter mobile app as part of the team. A client sign-in and a talk-to-sales path sit in the header. An FAQ covers the fund's philosophy, risk management, markets, minimum investment, fees and withdrawals, so most questions are answered before anyone reaches out.
