## The problem

Organic shapes make a page feel alive, but hand-drawn SVG blobs rarely share a point count, so morphing between them looks like a glitch instead of a breath.

## What I built

A small TypeScript function that turns a seed number into a smooth closed blob. Every shape it produces has the same structure, so any two of them tween cleanly. The blobs on this portfolio come straight out of it.

## How

Seeded random radii are placed around a circle, then joined with Catmull-Rom curves converted to cubic Béziers. A radial gradient offset toward the top left fakes a light source, which is enough to read as soft 3D without a single image or shadow.

## Result

Each blob costs a few hundred bytes of markup. The generator is under forty lines, has no dependencies and runs at build time, so visitors download shapes instead of code.
